Strategy rules
T4F Trap & Limit checks the last completed M15 candle, the H1 EMA 50 trend, and a completed M1 confirmation candle. Potential BUY and SELL levels use the strategy's digital-root offsets (12, 15, or 18 price units) from the recent 20-candle M15 swing levels. A valid entry must be within $5 of the level. Stop loss uses the swing level plus/minus 1.50; take profit is set at 3R. Scanning windows from the supplied code are 08:03–08:12 and 14:45–15:08 Lagos time. The feed fallback is labelled and does not generate trading signals. This dashboard generates signals only; it does not place broker orders.
Signals are rule-based and are not a guarantee of results. Confirm prices and lot specifications with your broker before trading.
